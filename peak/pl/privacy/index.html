Privacy policy

PEAK Daily Map is a static fan site. It has no server of its own, no accounts and no database — every page is a plain file, served as it is.

What is stored in your browser

Two preferences are kept in your browser's local storage: the language you picked and whether you switched to the dark theme. They stay on your device, are never sent anywhere and cannot be read by us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

What the host records

The site is served by GitHub Pages through Cloudflare. Like any web server, they log technical request data — IP address, browser user agent and the page requested — to deliver the traffic and to protect against abuse. That data is handled by GitHub and Cloudflare under their own privacy policies, not by this site.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings.

Your choices

You can clear the stored preferences at any time through your browser settings, and block cookies or use a content blocker. Nothing on this site requires you to identify yourself.

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the site's GitHub repository.
