Medicine

Medicine · 5.0 kg

What it does

Painkillers: cut 30% injury and 30% poison — and since the game removes spores alongside poison, 30% spores as well. Can be used on a friend.

Effects

  • Reduces 30% injury
  • Reduces 30% poison
  • Reduces 30% spores (poison takes the spores with it)
  • Can be used on a friend

Where to find it

Spawns on the ground — check the map layers to find it.
